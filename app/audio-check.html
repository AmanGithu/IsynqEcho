How it works on the web

Since Isynq's web app runs in your browser, it uses your microphone to hear what's being said in the meeting. That means a few things need to be set up.

  • Allow microphone access — click "Allow" when your browser asks.
  • Play audio through speakers — meeting audio needs to come out loud so the mic can pick it up.
  • Remove earphones — if audio goes to your ears, the mic won't hear the other participants in the meeting.
