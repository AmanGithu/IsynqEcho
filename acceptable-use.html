Acceptable Use Policy

Last Updated: May 1, 2026

1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy outlines the permitted and prohibited uses of the Isynq platform. All users must comply with this policy when using our services.

2. Permitted Uses

  • Using Isynq for personal interview preparation and real-time assistance
  • Using the Service during legitimate business meetings and calls
  • Uploading your own documents (resumes, notes, etc.) for AI context
  • Using the Screen Analyzer feature for coding and technical interviews
  • Customizing response formats and AI settings for your needs

3. Prohibited Uses

  • Using Isynq to cheat on academic examinations or certification tests
  • Recording or distributing meeting content without participant consent
  • Using the Service to impersonate another person
  • Uploading content that infringes on intellectual property rights
  • Attempting to bypass security measures or exploit vulnerabilities
  • Using the Service for fraudulent, deceptive, or illegal purposes
  • Reselling access to the Service without authorization
  • Using automated tools to abuse the free tier credits system

4. Ethical Use

We encourage users to use Isynq responsibly. While the tool helps you perform at your best, the answers you give should reflect your genuine knowledge and experience. Our AI generates suggestions based on your provided context — how you use those suggestions is your responsibility.

5. Reporting Violations

If you become aware of any violation of this policy, please report it to support@isynq.ai.

6. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in temporary suspension or permanent termination of your account. We reserve the right to take appropriate action at our sole discretion.

7. Changes

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated via email or through the Service.
