# ISYNQ Brand & Design Identity

## Visual Direction: "Lumina Professional"
The ISYNQ platform is built on a "Lumina" design system—a visual language that balances high-tech AI capability with human-centric accessibility. It uses a clean, light-mode foundation with high-contrast accents to evoke a sense of clarity, intelligence, and professional rigor.

### 1. Color Palette
The brand utilizes a sophisticated blue-centric palette that communicates trust and stability:
- **Primary:** `Brand Blue (#2563eb)` - Used for primary actions, active navigation states, and key interactive elements.
- **Surface:** `Lumina White (#f8f9ff)` - A cool-toned off-white that reduces eye strain while maintaining a crisp, modern feel.
- **Accent/Success:** `Emerald Green (#059669)` - Used for status indicators (Active), completion states, and "Start" actions to provide a secondary positive reinforcement.
- **Neutral:** A range of Slate Grays for text hierarchy and subtle borders, ensuring a professional "dashboard" feel.

### 2. Typography
- **Typeface:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`
- **Rationale:** A modern geometric sans-serif that offers excellent legibility at small sizes (labels/data) and a clean, tech-forward personality at larger sizes (headings).
- **Hierarchy:** Strong contrast between bold headlines and medium-weight body text helps users navigate dense information layouts quickly.

### 3. Layout Strategy
The application follows a consistent **Dual-Nav Architecture**:
- **Persistent Sidebar:** A 280px left-hand navigation rail providing instant access to high-level domains (Dashboard, AI Pilots, Settings).
- **Top AppBar:** A secondary header for contextual actions (Search, Notifications, Profile) and the primary "Start Session" CTA.
- **Content Canvas:** A flexible grid system that adapts to different data types—cards for the Assistants gallery, centered forms for Auth, and a split-screen high-focus layout for the Interview Window.

### 4. Component Language
- **Roundness:** `Round Eight (8px)` - Applied to cards, buttons, and inputs to create a "soft-pro" aesthetic that feels modern but not overly playful.
- **Elevation:** Minimal use of shadows (shadow-sm) to maintain a flat, clean "SaaS" look that emphasizes content over decoration.
- **Status Tags:** Capsule-shaped badges with soft background fills provide immediate visual feedback on the state of AI assistants (Active, Draft, Action Needed).

### 5. The "Interview Experience"
The core product differentiator—the AI Interview Window—is designed for **Deep Focus**:
- **Dark Mode Visualization:** The AI recruiter "Alex" is housed in a dark, high-contrast container to simulate a video call environment.
- **Voice-First Indicators:** Animated waveform visuals and prominent mic controls signal that the platform is listening and processing, reducing user anxiety during practice.

## User Experience (Feel)
ISYNQ feels **intuitive and empowering**. By mirroring common enterprise dashboard patterns, it lowers the learning curve, allowing users to focus entirely on their interview performance rather than navigating the tool. The transitions between "Management" (Assistants list) and "Execution" (Interview window) are designed to be seamless, creating a workflow that feels like a professional training gym.
